Why disagreement in biology should stay visible

A conflicting dataset is not automatically a bad dataset. Sometimes the disagreement is the biological signal telling you that context matters.

Biology rarely gives the same answer under every condition. A cell type can behave differently across developmental stage, culture system, perturbation, species, tissue environment or measurement platform.

Conflict can be information.

Suppose one dataset supports a proposed cell state while another does not. The useful question is not only which dataset is right. It is also what would have to be true for both observations to make sense.

Disagreement can be a hypothesis generator rather than a nuisance to average away.

Unknown deserves its own category.

If the relevant evidence was not measured well enough, the claim may simply be unresolved. That is different from evidence against it.
