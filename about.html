A note on purpose

Ontosma is an independent early-stage research effort, not a currently available research product.

Why build something like this?

There is an extraordinary amount we still don't understand about living systems. Not because we lack information alone, but because science also depends on choosing the right questions, interpreting experiments and seeing connections that aren't obvious at first.

We're interested in whether AI can meaningfully help with that thinking. Not by replacing the scientist, and not by treating every generated explanation as a discovery. The goal is a partner that can work carefully with evidence, suggest worthwhile alternatives and take criticism seriously.

Biology is broader than the next drug target.

Fundamental questions about development, ecosystems, evolution, cells and molecular systems all matter. Some become useful in ways no one could have predicted. Some help us respond to new diseases or other biological challenges. Others change our understanding of life for reasons that are valuable in themselves.

Ontosma's longer ambition is scientific preparedness through discovery: helping build the knowledge and intellectual tools that could matter when the unexpected happens.

The ambition is larger than the prototype.

The current work is an experimental, local research system. We are investigating evidence tracing, reproducible analysis, hypotheses, and scientific revision. It has not demonstrated original biological discovery, general reliability or consistent superiority to other capable AI systems.

We won't treat a good-looking website as evidence that those problems are solved. The research has to earn that claim.

Present status / October 2026

No public beta, signup list or release date. If the work becomes genuinely useful and ready for outside researchers, we will explain what it can do, where it fails and what access actually involves.
