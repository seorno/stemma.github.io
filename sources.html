Transparency

The photographs are real. They are not our results.

Plate 01 / Colonic tissue microscopy. From the scikit-image immunohistochemistry sample dataset. The picture shows colonic glands, with FHL2 protein detected using DAB and hematoxylin counterstaining. Acquired at the Center for Microscopy and Molecular Imaging (CMMI). The documentation states “No known copyright restrictions”; it does not specify a CC licence for this image. Original provenance and redistribution rights should be confirmed separately before release.

Plates 02 / Retinal fundus photography. From the scikit-image retina sample dataset, originally from Wikimedia. The documentation identifies the photograph as available under CC0 1.0 and references Mikael Häggström (2014), Medical gallery of Mikael Häggström 2014, WikiJournal of Medicine. The Approach page and Research Notes index use a wide crop of the photograph; the homepage uses a closer detail. Neither crop is an Ontosma result.

Images are bundled locally. Ontosma did not generate these micrographs. Some crops are made for layout, but the biological contents were not invented.

What this site is.

This is a static, research-stage website. It explains an independent project's motivation, design questions and intended standards. The research notes are editorial essays. The site does not offer scientific analyses, accounts, models or product access.

Privacy and functionality.

The website itself does not ask for personal information, store signups or use analytics or cookies. Its editorial fonts load through Google Fonts when there is an internet connection; that third-party service may receive the request. The site falls back to locally installed fonts if the service is unreachable.

Claims and corrections.

Ontosma has not established general scientific reliability, original biological discovery or consistent improvements over capable existing AI tools. Any future demonstration should include the question, method, evidence, limitations and an honest account of unsuccessful results.

This website is a local concept until its content, image terms, hosting arrangement and publication conditions are separately approved.
