The premise
behind Stemma

A biological conclusion should show what supports it, what contradicts it, and what remains unknown.

What Stemma makes visible

Not just an answer. The evidence around it.

Research teams often reach important decisions through scattered datasets, annotations, figures and judgement calls. Stemma is designed to bring that evidence into a structured view without pretending the biology is cleaner than it is.

01 / SUPPORT

Support

See where independent evidence converges on the same biological interpretation.

02 / CONTRADICTION

Contradiction

Keep disagreement visible instead of smoothing it away into a single confident-looking result.

03 / UNKNOWN

Unknown

Separate missing evidence from negative evidence so the next question is easier to define.

Research applications

Built around the questions researchers already ask.

The product is being developed for transcriptomic and cell-state work where conclusions need to survive comparison across datasets and biological contexts.

01 / CELL STATE

Interrogate state and lineage claims

Assess whether a proposed cell state, identity or transition is supported beyond a single marker set or inherited label.

Single-cell / developmental biology / differentiation
02 / CROSS-STUDY

Compare evidence across datasets

Place related studies into the same decision context and see where the underlying biological story is consistent, conditional or unresolved.

Replication / external validation / evidence review
03 / EXPERIMENTS

Clarify what is worth testing next

Use the structure of the available evidence to define the next experiment or validation question more deliberately.

Hypothesis review / experimental planning / prioritisation
04 / CONTEXT

Preserve biological context

Keep dataset conditions, disagreement and uncertainty attached to the conclusion rather than reducing them to one opaque score.

Traceability / context / scientific judgement
A research workflow

From a biological question to a defensible next step.

Stemma is intended to sit between raw biological evidence and the decision a scientist or R&D team has to make from it.

01 / FRAME

Frame

Start with a biological claim, state, comparison or experimental question.

02 / COMPARE

Compare

Bring relevant transcriptomic evidence into the same interpretive context.

03 / CHALLENGE

Challenge

Expose contradictions, missing evidence and assumptions that need attention.

04 / DECIDE

Decide

Carry a traceable rationale into the next experiment, review or R&D discussion.
