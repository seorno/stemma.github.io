Research applications

For questions where context changes the answer.

Stemma is being developed around transcriptomic and cell-state workflows where researchers need to understand whether a conclusion survives comparison beyond its original dataset.

01 / CELL STATE

Interrogate state and lineage claims.

Assess whether a proposed identity or transition remains plausible across markers, datasets and biological contexts.

02 / CROSS-STUDY

Compare evidence across studies.

Bring related evidence into one review surface while keeping the conditions that make studies different.

03 / EXPERIMENTS

Clarify what is worth testing next.

Use the structure of the available evidence to define a more deliberate next experiment or validation question.

The Stemma principle

Scientific judgement should become more inspectable, not more mysterious.

Stemma is not positioned as a machine that replaces the scientist. The product is built around making the evidence and uncertainty behind a decision easier to review.

01

Evidence over theatre.

Useful software should make the underlying reasoning easier to inspect, not distract with artificial confidence.

02

Context stays attached.

A conclusion should not outlive the assumptions and biological conditions that gave rise to it.

03

Uncertainty stays visible.

Knowing what has not been established can be as important as knowing what currently looks supported.
