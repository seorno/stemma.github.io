What Stemma enables

Less time assembling evidence. More time deciding what to do next.

Biology rarely gives one clean answer. Stemma is designed to help teams compare evidence, expose disagreement and move forward with a clearer view of what is known — and what is not.

Compare biological evidence

Bring results from multiple datasets into one decision context and see where the biological story converges or breaks apart.

Prioritize the next move
